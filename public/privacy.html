Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version. Your tasks are stored on your device. If you sign in with Microsoft, Google or Dropbox, they are synced to a folder in your own OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox that only Doozly uses. Doozly has no server that stores your tasks, and we don't use analytics, ads or trackers.

This policy covers the Doozly app at doozly.app, the Doozly desktop app and the Doozly relay at api.doozly.app. If you use a copy of Doozly that someone else hosts, their own policy applies.

Without an account

You can use Doozly without signing in. Your lists, tasks, steps, notes and settings are then stored only in your browser (or in the desktop app's data folder) on your device. We never receive them. Clearing your browser's site data for doozly.app deletes them.

When you sign in with Microsoft, Google or Dropbox

Signing in lets Doozly keep a copy of your tasks in your own cloud storage, so they appear on all your devices. Your browser talks to Microsoft, Google or Dropbox directly. Your tasks never pass through a Doozly server.

  • Microsoft: Doozly asks for access to its own app folder in your OneDrive (Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder), plus your name and email address to show which account you're signed in with. It cannot see any other files in your OneDrive.
  • Google: Doozly asks for access to its own hidden app data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), plus your name and email address to show which account you're signed in with. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Dropbox: Doozly asks for access to its own app folder in your Dropbox (Apps/Doozly, to read and write the files in it), plus your name and email address to show which account you're signed in with. It cannot see any other files in your Dropbox.

Your name and email address are kept on your device only.

Google API data

Doozly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google Drive app data folder is used only to sync your tasks between your devices. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not read by people.

The relay (api.doozly.app)

The relay is a small server that does two things a web page can't do on its own. It only runs when you sign in, and Doozly keeps working without it.

  • Reminders when the app is closed. Doozly sends the relay the time of each upcoming reminder, together with its text encrypted on your device. The key to decrypt it never leaves your devices, so the relay can't read your reminders. At the right time the relay sends the encrypted reminder through your browser's push service (run by Google, Mozilla, Apple or Microsoft, depending on your browser), and your device decrypts and shows it.
  • Staying signed in with Google. Google only lets a web app stay signed in if a server holds a secret, so the relay asks Google for a refresh token. That token is encrypted and stored in a cookie in your browser, not on the relay.
  • Staying signed in with Dropbox. Without the relay a Dropbox sign-in lasts 4 hours. With it, the relay asks Dropbox for a refresh token and keeps it the same way as Google's: encrypted, in a cookie in your browser.

To do this, the relay stores:

  • an account ID derived from your Microsoft, Google or Dropbox account (it doesn't reveal your email or the provider's own ID),
  • your browser's push subscription (the address and keys your browser's push service gives out),
  • your pending reminders: their time and their encrypted text.

A reminder is deleted once it has been delivered, or an hour after its time if it couldn't be. Signing out removes this device's push subscription. Backups of the relay's database are kept for 14 days.

The relay sets two cookies, both used only for signing in: a session cookie (30 days) and, if you sign in with Google or Dropbox, the encrypted refresh token (180 days). The relay's logs don't contain IP addresses, cookies or request contents.

Hosting

The website and the relay run with hosting and network providers. Like any website host, they process your IP address and technical request data to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. We don't use their analytics or anyone else's.

What we don't do

No analytics, no ads, no trackers, no selling or sharing of your data. We don't read your tasks, and we can't.

Your choices

  • Delete your tasks on a device: clear the site data for doozly.app in your browser, or uninstall the desktop app and remove its data.
  • Delete the synced copy: in OneDrive, delete the Apps/Doozly folder. In Google Drive, open Settings, then Manage apps, find Doozly and choose "Delete hidden app data". In Dropbox, delete the Apps/Doozly folder.
  • Remove Doozly's access: at account.live.com/consent/Manage for a personal Microsoft account, myapplications.microsoft.com for a work or school account, or myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google, or dropbox.com/account/connected_apps for Dropbox.
  • Delete what the relay holds: signing out stops reminders to that device, and pending reminders are deleted after their time anyway. To have everything removed at once, email us.

Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your personal data, such as access, correction and deletion. Email us to use them.

Children

Doozly is not aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
