# Changelog

What's new in each desktop release. The web app updates itself and isn't versioned. `scripts/release.sh` turns Unreleased into the new version.

## Unreleased

- First desktop app for Linux, macOS and Windows: native reminders, the tray, open at login, To Do's keyboard shortcuts and a quick add box (Ctrl+Alt+N, or ⌥⌘N on a Mac).
- Updates itself: a new version downloads in the background and installs when you restart.
- Sign in with Microsoft, Google or Dropbox to sync through your own OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox.
- The close button closes Doozly. To keep it running in the tray instead, so reminders still come, turn on "Keep running in the tray when closed" in Settings.
